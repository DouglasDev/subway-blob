Showtime is the acrobat’s original music loop. The musician alternates between five original loops, Off-Key, Crooked Waltz, Tin Can Blues, Cardboard Tango, and Wrong Stop. The pigeon gag and the three power-up sounds are original synthesized recordings. Pickup, pop, Cool Pack, and Quiet Bubble cues are generated by the game.

Each encounter plays a one-shot vocal reaction from a nearby rider two seconds after it starts in the car. These Freesound recordings are [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The game uses trimmed, filtered, peak-normalized mono MP3s.
