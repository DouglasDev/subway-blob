# Audio credits

The following NYC field recordings were obtained from their public Freesound previews. The source pages mark each recording [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The game bundles the edited mono MP3s and plays them offline.

Showtime is the acrobat’s original music loop. The musician alternates between five original loops, Off-Key, Crooked Waltz, Tin Can Blues, Cardboard Tango, and Wrong Stop. The pigeon gag and the three power-up sounds are original synthesized recordings. Pickup, pop, Cool Pack, and Quiet Bubble cues are generated by the game.

Each encounter plays a one-shot vocal reaction from a nearby rider two seconds after it starts in the car. These Freesound recordings are [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The game uses trimmed, filtered, peak-normalized mono MP3s.

The disruptive loudspeaker uses [Last stop.wav by jerk01](https://freesound.org/people/jerk01/sounds/86938/), a CC0 recording of an MTA R160 announcement. The game uses the 5.12–8.82 s excerpt with a narrow band-pass, distortion, and short echoes. Train jolts, sticky soda, the shove, rhino bumps, Super Fluid, and the expanded pop are synthesized in the game. Background rider reactions reuse the credited recordings at lower volume.

The two Brooklyn-accent reactions use [WAS THAT ME OR ONE OF YOU GUYS.wav by metrostock99](https://freesound.org/people/metrostock99/sounds/514704/) under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The game uses the 2.34–4.53 s and 6.25–8.61 s excerpts, trimmed, filtered, and faded to mono.
